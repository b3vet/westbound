# M2 playtest: Car, controls, cameras

**Gate (spec M2):** the lane-change time, settling and stability tests pass (they do, in CI), and **the drag and gyro layouts both feel precise on device**.

**Build:** the start scene is `src/dev/car_drive.tscn`. You drive the physics car on the empty procedural highway. There is no traffic, collision or scoring yet; leaving the carriageway puts the car back in a lane.

## Controls
- **Drag steering:** put a thumb down anywhere (in auto mode) and slide left or right. A small ring marks the anchor. Past about 2.5 cm the anchor follows your thumb. In auto mode: drag down to brake, flick up to boost.
- **Gyro steering:** tilt like a steering wheel. Tap **STEER** to switch, then tap the screen once more and **accept Safari's motion prompt**. **RECAL** sets the current tilt as neutral. In auto mode: hold anywhere to brake, swipe up to boost.
- **Manual throttle** (**THR**): hold the gas pedal (bottom right); the brake is proportional to how high your thumb sits on the pedal. Boost button above gas.
- **MIRROR** switches to left-handed layouts. **CAR** cycles Falcon GT, Night Viper and Brute V8. **RESET** puts the car back in its lane. **CAM** cycles chase, far, hood and overhead. **HUD** shows the dev HUD; **COPY** in it gives a report to paste back.
- Desktop: A/D steer, W gas (manual), S brake, Shift boost, C camera.
