# M3 playtest: Traffic

**Gate (spec M3):** the 10,000 km soak, determinism and fairness tests pass (WP3.3 is running them), and **a 10-minute sandbox review shows readable, lively traffic**.

**Builds** (https://b3vet.github.io/westbound/):
- **Drive:** you in traffic at leg 3 density. `DEV +` opens the dev buttons: LEG cycles density 1–8; the layout, car, wheel/ring and size buttons are there too. Hits follow the lives rules (a 2 s ghost, a deflection, and the hit car swerves with hazards on), but lives are infinite until Phase 4. There is no score HUD yet.
- **Sandbox:** `?scene=sandbox`, or `DEV +` → SANDBOX. It has a free or top-down camera, pause/step/time scale, spawn controls and overlays (IDM gaps, MOBIL decisions, blinker timers, the no-ambush zone). Tap a car to see its lane-change decision.

## Traffic (10 minutes, sandbox and drive)
- [ ] **Readable:** can you tell what each car is about to do (blinkers about 1 s before moving, brake lights, hazards)? Is it readable into the low sun at golden hour and sunset?
- [ ] **Fair:** did anything ambush you, cut into your space, brake unexpectedly hard or appear out of nowhere?
- [ ] **Alive:** trucks and buses keep right, aggressive cars pass you from behind, the odd motorbike, cruisers in the right lane. Enough variety?
- [ ] **Density:** LEG 1 → 8. Does leg 8 feel dense but passable? (Leg 8 hits the 60-car cap: plan D7.)
- [ ] **Night:** head- and taillights, brake lights, headlight pools on the road.

## Controls (M2 feedback round 2)

- [ ] **WHEEL:** is the 135° turn and 2.4 cm size right?
